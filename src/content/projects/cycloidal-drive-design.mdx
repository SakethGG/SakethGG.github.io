Two documented cycloidal drive designs at a 20:1 reduction ratio: a compact version optimised for
efficiency and durability, and a larger standard version. Both are fully documented with CAD files
and explanations of the working principle, and the geometry is optimised for 3D printing so it
drops into other projects without modification.

The plan was to follow up with a Python-based cycloid gear graphic calculator — interactive profile
visualization, adjustable parameters, DXF export — as a port of an existing tool with a better
interface. That part is still on the shelf; for now, [GearSolver](https://github.com/dsuarezv/GearSolver)
covers the same ground.
