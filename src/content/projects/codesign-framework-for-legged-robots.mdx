A 2-DOF planar leg testbed for co-designing robot link lengths `[L1, L2]` against a torque/tracking
cost, following De Vincenti, Kang & Coors (ICRA 2023). It couples two nested optimization loops:

- **Inner loop** — a per-timestep QP (solved via OSQP) computes joint accelerations and torques
  jointly for an operational-space PD task-tracking controller, simulated forward in MuJoCo.
- **Outer loop** — LBFGS++ optimizes the link lengths against the accumulated cost (squared mean
  actuator effort), using gradients from analytic KKT differentiation of the QP — an OptNet-style
  implicit differentiation of the inner controller — plus finite-difference-verified dynamics
  partials.

The interesting part is the KKT sensitivity: instead of treating the inner-loop controller as a
black box and finite-differencing through the whole simulation, the gradient of the outer cost
with respect to the design variables is propagated analytically through the QP's optimality
conditions.
