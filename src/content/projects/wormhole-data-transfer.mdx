I made this project specifically to transfer files from my work desktop to my laptop really quickly. I also need it to send messages: if I write text in the portal, it should instantly pop up on the screen of the other desk setup.

I have a lot of files on my Mac and some more on my desktop PC. I need them on either of them. For file transfers like PDFs and images, I had to use either WhatsApp or Gmail to send them to the other setup, and after sending them I again had to open the mail or WhatsApp Web and download them. So I made this small tool to make my life easier and faster.

## Wormhole-Data-Transfer

This is a small pop-up icon which stays on your monitor at all times, or you can open it whenever you need it. It is very small and can be placed anywhere on your screen. Whenever you need a file from one system on another system, you just have to drag it and drop it on the icon, and it will instantly send it to its partner computer.

The other feature is messaging, to send text and other details really quickly. It will instantly send it to the other machine. You can also use this feature with a friend who is on the same network to transfer files and chat. The message will pop up for 5 seconds and disappear; if you want to read it again, you can just click on the icon and it shows your past texts and other items. This is extremely handy, as it is on your desktop and your friend or teammate can instantly see it on their monitor, so you can get a quick response.

You can also drag and drop your Safari/Chrome tabs into it, and it will instantly open the same link on the other machine.

I use it every single day, and it definitely makes my life easier.
