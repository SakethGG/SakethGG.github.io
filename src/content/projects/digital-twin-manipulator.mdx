A MATLAB/Simulink digital twin of the UR5 six-DOF manipulator, covering forward and inverse
kinematics, rigid-body dynamics, LuGre friction modelling, trajectory generation through Cartesian
waypoints, and 3D animation of the resulting motion.

The goal was a simulation model faithful enough to be used for offline trajectory validation,
controller tuning, and torque/friction analysis before running anything on the real arm — kinematics
and dynamics through MATLAB's Robotics System Toolbox, friction and position control through
Simscape Multibody.
