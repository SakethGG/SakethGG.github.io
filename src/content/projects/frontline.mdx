Built because I couldn't keep up with the papers coming out every day. Frontline searches the web for
new work in my research interests and from professors I follow, merges and filters out what I've
already seen, and uses Claude/GPT to summarise and score each paper. It also holds a library of saved
papers, tracks conference deadlines, and gives me a place to plan projects and jot down future research
ideas. Everything runs locally on my machine, so none of my data leaves it.
