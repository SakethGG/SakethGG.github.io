A chapter-wise collection of MATLAB scripts for building intuition around robotics fundamentals —
spatial transformations, manipulator kinematics and inverse kinematics, Jacobians, dynamics,
trajectory planning, and linear/nonlinear/force control — following John Craig's *Introduction to
Robotics* and Bruno Siciliano's *Robotics: Modelling, Planning and Control*, with each script paired
with a step-by-step animation rather than just equations on a page.

Still a work in progress, chapter by chapter.
