Open-source scripts for driving a Moteus brushless motor controller from a Raspberry Pi 4 over
pi3hat, aimed at robotics applications that need precise torque, velocity, and position control.
Includes motor characterisation scripts for pulling out the parameters needed before trusting a
motor in closed-loop control.
