A replica of the actuator used in the MIT Mini Cheetah, the small quadruped that made
quasi-direct-drive actuation popular for agile legged robots. The design goals are the same as the
original: high torque density, fast response and efficient power delivery in a short, flat
package that can be bolted straight onto a leg. That makes it a good fit for quadrupeds, bipeds,
exoskeletons and robotic arms, where you want a lot of torque without much weight.

![](/images/projects/mini-cheetah-actuator/isometric_view.png)

The layout follows the Mini Cheetah: a large-diameter brushless outrunner motor with a low-ratio
planetary reduction packed inside it, so the gearbox adds almost no length. The low ratio keeps the
actuator backdrivable, which means the motor current is a good stand-in for output torque and the
leg can absorb impacts without force sensors. Wherever I could, I designed around off-the-shelf
motor and gear components to keep it cheap and easy to build.

![](/images/projects/mini-cheetah-actuator/side_view.png)

The control side was planned around field-oriented control (FOC) on an embedded C++ driver, with
encoder, temperature and real-time feedback for precise torque control.

## Components

The gearbox is a single 6:1 planetary stage (100-tooth ring / 20-tooth sun + 1) built from
off-the-shelf KHK module 0.5 gears.

| Component | Qty | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Front housing | 1 | Custom, holds the ring gear and output bearing |
| Back housing with controller housing | 1 | Custom, carries the stator and motor driver |
| Back plate | 1 | Custom |
| Stator | 1 | Brushless motor stator with windings |
| Rotor | 1 | Outrunner rotor with magnets, drives the sun gear |
| Sun gear holder | 1 | Custom, couples the rotor to the sun gear |
| KHK GEAB0.5-20-8-K-4 | 1 | Sun gear, module 0.5, 20 teeth |
| KHK GEFHB0.5-40-5-8-W3-H10 | 3 | Planet gears, module 0.5, 40 teeth |
| KHK SI0.5-100 | 1 | Internal ring gear, module 0.5, 100 teeth |
| Planetary top plate | 1 | Custom planet carrier, output side |
| Planetary bottom plate | 1 | Custom planet carrier |
| 4 x 20 mm dowel pin | 3 | Planet shafts |
| HK0408 needle bearing | 3 | Planet gear bearings |
| 6708 ball bearing | 1 | Thin-section output bearing |
| 6702 ball bearing | 2 | Thin-section rotor/carrier bearings |
| 624H ball bearing | 1 | Small support bearing |

The repository has the full CAD: SolidWorks part and assembly files plus STEP exports. I put it
together so students, researchers and developers can see how the Mini Cheetah actuator is built
and use it as a starting point for their own.
